🔒 Privacy Policy — Fahm-ul-Quran

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary (English)

Fahm-ul-Quran is a free Quran word-learning app. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Your name and learning progress are stored only on your own device. The app contacts third-party services only to fetch prayer times (your approximate coordinates, only if you use that feature), Quran text/audio, and fonts. We do not sell or share personal data. For any question or data request, write to gormefauzan@gmail.com.

1. Kaunsi Maloomat Use Hoti Hai

a) Jo aap khud dete hain:

  • Naam — login ke waqt, sirf aapka profile banane ke liye. Ye aapke device par save hota hai.
  • Login code — app ka access code (password) aap daalte hain. Ye humare kisi server par nahi bheja jaata aur hum koi email, phone number ya photo nahi maangte.
  • Location — sirf agar aap "Azan ka waqt / Namaz" feature use karein aur phone ki permission dein (optional). Permission na dein to app baaki sab chalta rahega.

b) Aapka progress:

  • Kaunsa din, kaunse lafz yaad hain, exam ke score, namaz ka record, favourites, settings.
  • Ye sab aapke device par (browser localStorage / app storage) mein save hota hai. Humare server par kuch nahi jaata, kyunke humara koi server hai hi nahi.
  • Backup file: aap chahein to progress ki ek file (.json) apne phone mein save kar sakte hain aur naye phone par restore kar sakte hain. Ye file sirf aapke paas rehti hai; hum use nahi dekhte.
  • Agar aap Quran audio offline download karein, to wo bhi aapke device par (IndexedDB / cache) mein rakhi jaati hai.

2. Third-Party Services (Data Kahan Jaata Hai)

3. Android App ki Permissions

  • Location — namaz ke auqaat ke liye (optional)
  • Notifications — azan/namaz ki yaad dahani ke liye
  • Internet — Quran text/audio aur namaz ke auqaat laane ke liye
  • Alarm / boot complete / wake lock / foreground service — azan alarm waqt par bajane aur phone restart ke baad dobara set karne ke liye, aur audio chalane ke liye

Ye permissions sirf upar likhe kaam ke liye hain. Koi analytics, ads ya tracking SDK app mein nahi hai.

4. Aapke Haq

  • Sab kuch delete karein — Logout karein, ya app/browser ka data clear karein. Sab kuch aapke device par hai, isliye wahi se hat jaata hai.
  • Location band karein — phone settings se permission hata dein, app phir bhi chalega.
  • Sawal ya shikayat — gormefauzan@gmail.com

5. Bachchon ki Privacy

App sabhi umron ke liye hai. Hum jaan-boojh kar bachchon se koi personal data collect nahi karte. Login mein sirf naam hota hai, aur wo bhi sirf device par rehta hai.

6. Ads aur Purchases

Koi ads nahi hain. Koi asli paise ka in-app purchase nahi hai. App ke andar ke inaam sirf XP (points) se milte hain jo aap padh kar kamate hain.

7. Deeni Disclaimer

Zaroori: Ye app kisi mustanad Aalim, Qari ya Mufti ki jagah nahi hai. Tajweed, namaz ka tareeqa aur deeni ahkam kisi mustanad ustad se seekhein. App ka text/audio sirf seekhne mein madad ke liye hai. Kisi bhi deeni faisle se pehle apne aalim se rujoo karein. Certificate sirf app mein "completion" ki tasdeeq hai, koi fatwa ya alimiyat ki sanad nahi.
